HomeschoolHub

HomeschoolHub is a private family organizer for one household: a shared calendar, chores, lists and the homeschool day. It runs on a computer in our home, not on a cloud service.

When a parent connects a Google account, HomeschoolHub keeps that person's Google Calendar and the family calendar in sync, so events added in either place show up in both.

It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
