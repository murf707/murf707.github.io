Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

HomeschoolHub is a private organizer used by one family. It runs on a computer in that family's home.

What it accesses from Google

If you choose to connect your Google account, HomeschoolHub asks for permission to:

  • see the list of Google calendars you have, so you can pick which ones to sync;
  • view, create, change and delete events on those calendars, so the family calendar and your Google Calendar stay in step.

It does not access your email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

  • Calendar events are stored only on the family's own computer, to show them on the family calendar.
  • The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on that computer.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • No one outside the household can see the data.

HomeschoolHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect at any time in HomeschoolHub's settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When you disconnect, the stored token and synced copies of your Google events are deleted from the computer.
